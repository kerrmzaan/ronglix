Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 August 2026 · Last updated: 4 August 2026 · Version 2026.08

Plain-language summary. Ronglix software is built to be local-first: most data you create lives only on your device. Our website ronglix.com does not set advertising cookies and does not sell your data. Our six published mobile apps integrate ad mediation platforms (Google AdMob and others listed below) which may collect device-level identifiers and contextual signals for ad selection, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. We never knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under the higher age defined by your country). You can contact us at contact@ronglix.com to exercise any privacy right.

1. Scope & data controller

This Privacy Policy applies to (a) the website ronglix.com and all subdomains; (b) the six mobile applications published by Ronglix on the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and any other platform — namely Ronglix Workflow, Ronglix Collector, Ronglix Pulse, Ronglix Vault, Ronglix Notes, and Ronglix Budget; and (c) any beta or pre-release builds distributed through TestFlight, Google Play Internal Testing, Apple Business Manager, or direct download.

The data controller for the website and the apps is:

Ronglix Studio Ltd.
University of Stirling Innovation Park
Stirling, Scotland, FK9 4LA, United Kingdom

Business support: support@ronglix.com

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at contact@ronglix.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". We aim to acknowledge every privacy request within 72 hours and resolve within the statutory 30-day window.

2. What we collect

2.1 Website ronglix.com

  • Server logs — IP address, user-agent, request URL, HTTP status, and timestamp, retained for 14 days for security and abuse prevention.
  • Aggregate analytics — privacy-respecting, cookie-less page-view counts (Plausible-compatible protocol). No personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting.
  • Email correspondences — when you email contact@ronglix.com or support@ronglix.com, we receive your email address, message content, and any attachments.
  • Newsletter subscription — name (optional), email address, declared role. You can unsubscribe via the link in any email.

2.2 Mobile applications

Because our apps are local-first, most of the data you create stays on your device. When an integrated ad mediation platform serves an ad, the SDKs running on your device may receive and process the following signals — never the underlying personal content you have stored in the app:

  • Device identifiers: Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), Vendor ID, App Set ID, and the developer's hashed device fingerprint (only when the Advertising ID is unavailable, e.g. when the user has reset it).
  • Device properties: device model, OS version, locale, language, time zone, screen size, carrier, network type (Wi-Fi / cellular), free storage, free RAM, battery level.
  • App metadata: app version, build number, session start/end timestamps, session length, screen flow (with hashed screen names).
  • Ad interaction signals: ad request, ad impression, ad click, ad completion (for rewarded video), and whether the ad was skipped.
  • Crash and performance metrics: anonymised stack traces, frame rates, and request latencies. No screenshots, no screen recordings.

The SDKs we integrate for advertising and analytics purposes are listed in detail in section 12. Their respective privacy policies govern their independent data handling.

3. Why we collect it

  • To operate, secure, and debug our services.
  • To respond to your enquiries when you contact us.
  • To deliver advertising — and only the minimum signals required for ad selection, frequency capping, fraud detection, and reward validation.
  • To comply with applicable law, including the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

We do not use the data we collect to: build advertising profiles across other companies' apps or websites, sell your data to third parties, infer sensitive personal attributes, or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal basis under GDPR / UK GDPR

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for keeping our services secure, preventing fraud, basic aggregate analytics, and serving contextual advertising.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalised / behavioural advertising. On iOS 14.5+ and Android 13+ we honour the platform's ATT / ad-id reset controls; if you decline, we serve only contextual ads.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for responding to your enquiries and fulfilling any paid engagement.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — for compliance with the EU Digital Services Act, UK age-appropriate design code, and tax/accounting record-keeping.

5. Retention & deletion

  • Server logs: 14 days, then aggregated or deleted.
  • Email correspondences: up to 24 months for business records, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Newsletter list: until you unsubscribe.
  • Mobile app ad data: controlled by the respective ad SDK; typically capped at 13–18 months.
  • Data you create inside our apps: stays on your device until you delete the app or use the in-app "erase all data" feature.

You may request immediate deletion of any data we hold about you by emailing contact@ronglix.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

7. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA — for example to ad-mediation partners in the United States — we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or equivalent safeguards. Where transfers occur to a country without an adequacy decision, we conduct a Transfer Risk Assessment and apply supplementary measures (encryption in transit and at rest, pseudonymisation, contractual purpose limitation).

8. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards:

  • TLS 1.3 for all data in transit; HSTS preload.
  • AES-256 encryption at rest; Argon2id for password-derived keys.
  • Multi-factor authentication on all administrative accounts.
  • Annual third-party penetration testing; quarterly dependency audits.
  • Documented incident-response plan with 72-hour breach notification under GDPR Art. 33.
  • Restricted access on a need-to-know basis; access logs retained for 12 months.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have some or all of the following rights:

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict or object to processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing.
  • Right to opt-out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising (CCPA/CPRA).
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for EEA users, your national Data Protection Authority; for UK users, the ICO (ico.org.uk); for California users, the California Privacy Protection Agency.

To exercise any right, email contact@ronglix.com. We respond within 30 days; usually much sooner.

12. Ad format compliance

Our applications integrate the following advertising formats. Each format is configured to comply with the Apple App Store Guidelines, Google Play Developer Policy, and the EU DSA, with specific protections for children (see section 17).

12.1 Splash / app-open ads

  • Purpose: short, full-screen ad displayed immediately after the user opens the app.
  • Frequency cap: maximum one (1) splash ad per session, with a 24-hour cool-down between subsequent splash ads for the same user.
  • Skip option: a clearly labelled "close" / "skip" affordance appears within 5 seconds, compliant with EU DSA Art. 28 and Google's "Choose Ads" controls.
  • Children: splash ads are not served in child-directed builds (COPPA / UK Age-Appropriate Design Code).

12.2 Rewarded video ads

  • Purpose: the user opts in to watch a full-length video ad in exchange for an in-app reward (e.g. an extra feature unlock, virtual currency).
  • Frequency cap: maximum six (6) rewarded videos per user per day, per app.
  • Consent: opt-in only — never auto-played. We display a clear disclosure: "Watch this ad to earn 1 credit" before the user initiates playback.
  • Children: rewarded video is disabled in child-directed builds and in jurisdictions that prohibit in-app reward-for-ad mechanics (e.g. China).

12.3 Interstitial ads

  • Purpose: full-screen ad displayed at natural transition points (e.g. after completing a workflow, returning to home).
  • Frequency cap: no more than one interstitial per 90 seconds of active use; never back-to-back.
  • Skip option: a clearly visible "close" affordance within 5 seconds of display.
  • Children: interstitial ads in child-directed builds are static, contextual, and never interactive.

12.4 Banner ads

  • Purpose: small, persistent ad strip shown at the top or bottom of selected screens.
  • Placement: never overlaps content, controls, or input fields; honours the platform's safe-area insets.
  • Refresh rate: capped at 30–60 seconds to minimise battery and data usage.
  • Children: banner ads are not served in child-directed builds.

12.5 Other ad formats

  • Native ads — clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or "Ad".
  • Playable ads (try-before-you-install mini-game ads) — opt-in only; never auto-launched.
  • Offerwall ads — disabled in child-directed builds and in EU under-18 profiles.
  • Push notification ads — not used. Ronglix apps never send push notifications for the purpose of advertising.

13. Google AdMob — specifics

Google AdMob is our primary mediation platform. AdMob acts as a data processor on our behalf under Google's Ads Data Processing Terms. The following specifics apply:

13.1 SDK and runtime configuration

  • iOS SDK: Google Mobile Ads SDK (currently 11.x series), integrated via CocoaPods or Swift Package Manager. IDFA usage gated behind Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework where required by jurisdiction.
  • Android SDK: Google Mobile Ads SDK (currently 23.x series), integrated via Gradle. Google Advertising ID (GAID) usage honours the user's "Opt out of personalised ads" toggle.
  • UMP consent: we use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to surface a TCF v2.2-compliant consent dialog for EEA / UK users, and a CPRA-compliant "Limited Use of Personal Information" notice for California users.

13.2 Data categories processed by AdMob

  • Device identifiers (IDFA / GAID, when permission granted).
  • Device-level technical metadata (model, OS version, locale, screen, network).
  • Ad interaction events (request, impression, click, completion).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS).
  • App-set ID and session identifiers.

13.3 AdMob Restricted Data Processing

For users under 18, or for users who explicitly request restricted processing, we set the tagForUnderAgeOfConsent flag and disable personalised advertising in line with Google's Families policies.

13.4 Families Policy & Designed for Families

For any Ronglix app designated as Designed for Families (Google Play) or in the Kids category (Apple App Store), we configure AdMob to serve only contextual ads, no interest-based categories, and no mediated demand from networks that do not self-certify compliance with the Families Self-Certification programme.

13.5 User controls

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" — Ronglix apps respect this immediately.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of personalised ads.
  • Cross-platform reset: any time, in our app under Settings → Privacy → Reset Advertising ID.

14. Ad mediation partners & compliance

Each ad network integrated into our mediation stack operates as an independent data controller for the data it collects. Each has been assessed for compliance with:

  • Apple App Store Guidelines (especially Guidelines 1.4, 5.1.1, 5.1.2 — Kids Category).
  • Google Play Developer Policy (especially the Ads policy and Families policy).
  • IAB Tech Lab's OpenRTB 2.6 and TCF v2.2 specifications.
  • EU DSA Art. 28 (recommender system transparency) and Art. 39 (online advertising transparency).
  • EU DSA Statement of Reasons (Ad Repository) requirements — for any campaigns targeting the EU.

You can view the live SDK list inside our apps under Settings → Privacy → Ad Partners. The list mirrors the inventory reported to Google Play Data Safety and Apple App Privacy Labels.

15. App store compliance

15.1 Apple App Store

Each Ronglix app submitted to the Apple App Store includes a completed Privacy Label (App Privacy Details on App Store Connect) that accurately declares the data collected and the tracking performed. We update this declaration whenever the data practices change.

We comply with:

  • App Store Review Guidelines 1.4 (Safety) and 5.1 (Privacy).
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — IDFA is not collected without consent.
  • EU Digital Services Act — DSA-compliant transparency for ad targeting and recommender systems.
  • EU Digital Markets Act — App Tracking Transparency is supported on iOS.
  • EU DMA browser engine and choice-screen requirements (when applicable).

15.2 Google Play Store

Each Ronglix app submitted to Google Play includes a completed Data Safety form declaring data collection, sharing, and security practices. We comply with:

  • Google Play Developer Policy (User Data, Privacy, and Ads sections).
  • Google Play Families Policy — for any app in the Designed for Families programme.
  • Data Safety section — accurate disclosure of data collected, shared, and required.
  • EU DSA — declarations in line with the EU repository requirements.

15.3 Apple App Store — Kids Category

Apps in the Apple App Store's Kids Category are configured to:

  • Not include behavioural advertising.
  • Not include third-party analytics.
  • Not transmit data to third parties other than the app publisher.
  • Include only contextual ads via mediation partners certified under Apple's Kids Category programme.

15.4 Huawei AppGallery / Amazon Appstore / Samsung Galaxy Store

For builds distributed on alternative storefronts, we apply the same privacy baseline as the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. SDKs that are not available on a given platform are not bundled.

16. Country-specific & regional policies

The Ronglix policy is designed to satisfy the strictest applicable privacy regime in any jurisdiction we ship to. The following country-specific sections describe how our policy maps to local law and what additional rights you may have.

16.1 European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom — GDPR / UK GDPR

We process personal data under one of the six lawful bases in Art. 6(1) GDPR, summarised in section 4. We honour the rights listed in section 9. Our designated supervisory authority for EEA users is your national Data Protection Authority; for UK users, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO, ico.org.uk).

We honour the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension, and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for any transfer of personal data outside the EEA / UK.

16.2 California (USA) — CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect and the categories of sources.
  • Know whether we sell or share your personal information, and to whom.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights.

We do not sell personal information for money. We may "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by CPRA. You may opt out by toggling "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS, "Opt out of personalised ads" on Android, or emailing contact@ronglix.com with the subject "CPRA Opt-Out".

16.3 United States — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Apps designated for children on either storefront follow the rules of:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).
  • Apple's Kids Category guidelines.
  • Google Play's Designed for Families programme.
  • California SB-1424 (Protecting Our Kids from Social Media Addiction Act — for applicable age ranges).

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we delete it within 30 days. Parents may contact contact@ronglix.com to review or request deletion of their child's information.

16.4 European Union — Digital Services Act (DSA)

Where the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065) applies to our services, we:

  • Publish annual transparency reports on content moderation (none to date for Ronglix).
  • Surface clear labelling of advertisements and the parameters used to select them.
  • Maintain a single, public Statement of Reasons API endpoint for ad-related queries.
  • Provide researchers vetted access to non-personal data where feasible.
  • Implement a user-friendly notice-and-action mechanism for illegal content.

16.5 Canada — PIPEDA & Quebec Law 25

We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, Quebec's Law 25. You may request access to and correction of your personal information by emailing contact@ronglix.com. Complaints may be addressed to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

16.6 Brazil — LGPD

We comply with the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD). Data subjects in Brazil have rights similar to those under GDPR, including access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion, and confirmation of processing. The ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados) is the supervisory authority.

16.7 Asia-Pacific — APPI / PDPA / PIPL

  • Japan (APPI): we comply with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information. We honour disclosure, correction, and cessation-of-use requests.
  • South Korea (PIPA): we comply with the Personal Information Protection Act. Cross-border transfers occur only with your consent or under equivalent safeguards.
  • Singapore (PDPA): we comply with the Personal Data Protection Act. We participate in the Do Not Call registry if you opt in.
  • Australia (Privacy Act 1988): we comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).
  • China (PIPL): we comply with the Personal Information Protection Law where applicable; cross-border transfers follow CAC security assessment or Standard Contract requirements.
  • India (DPDP Act 2023): we comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and honour data principal rights.

17. Children's privacy

Ronglix takes the privacy of children very seriously. Our practices:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the higher age defined by your country's law, e.g. under 14 in Spain, South Korea, and the UK; under 16 in France and the Netherlands; under 18 in certain US states for certain features).
  • For any app in the Apple App Store Kids Category or Google Play Designed for Families programme, we configure all SDKs (analytics, ads, attribution) to comply with the strictest applicable rules.
  • We do not serve personalised (interest-based) advertising to children.
  • We do not enable chat, user-generated content, or social features for children without parental consent and clear disclosure.
  • We do not use nudge techniques or persuasive design aimed at prolonging children's engagement (UK Age-Appropriate Design Code best practice).
  • Parents / guardians may request access, correction, or deletion of any information collected from their child by emailing contact@ronglix.com with the subject "Children's Privacy". We respond within 5 business days.
