// FAQ

Quick answers

The questions we get asked most often — answered up front.

What size of team do you take?

Solo founders, indie studios of 3–12, and product teams of 20–60. We rarely work with agencies — we prefer to be your external team, not a sub-contractor.

Do you do equity / sweat equity?

Occasionally, for the right early-stage studio with a sharp problem and a credible founder. We take cash-first by default — equity only after trust is built.

What's your typical response time?

One working day during UK business hours. We answer every email ourselves — no auto-responders, no SDR funnels.

Can you sign an NDA before we chat?

Yes — our standard mutual NDA is one click away. For deeper confidentiality we use PGP and Signal.

Do you maintain the code afterwards?

Yes — our Care Plan covers OS updates, store submissions, dependency upgrades, and a small monthly feature budget. We don't disappear after launch.

Where do you work?

University of Stirling Innovation Park in Scotland. UK timezone, EU holidays. Most collaboration is async, with weekly live demos.

Do you publish code?

We open-source internal tools when they're useful to others (DSP graphs, motion libraries). Client code stays private unless you choose otherwise.

How do you handle IP?

Full source code and IP transfer at the end of every engagement. We sign your agreement, not ours. We're not in the business of owning your work.
